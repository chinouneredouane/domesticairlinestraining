Module 8 – Causes of Airplane Upset

⏱ Time: 0m 0s

Airplane upsets are rare but serious events. They can be triggered by three main categories:

  • Environmentally Induced: Turbulence, windshear, microbursts, icing, and wake turbulence can cause sudden attitude changes or control loss.
  • System Induced: Automation anomalies, flight control malfunctions, or sensor errors may lead to unexpected aircraft behavior.
  • Pilot Induced: Incorrect inputs, distraction, or misinterpretation of flight data can escalate into an upset condition.
