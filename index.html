Introduction

Upset Prevention and Recovery Training (UPRT) is essential to reduce Loss of Control In-flight (LOC-I). This module follows ICAO Doc 10011 and supports competency-based training for B738 crews.

Causes of Upset

Upsets may result from environmental factors (turbulence, windshear), system anomalies, or pilot inputs. Understanding these helps prevent escalation.

Recognition

Early recognition is key. Indicators include excessive pitch, bank angles, or airspeed deviations. Pilots must maintain situational awareness and monitor flight parameters.

Recovery Techniques

Recovery involves reducing angle of attack, leveling wings, and managing thrust. Techniques vary by upset type: nose-high, nose-low, or roll-induced.

Summary & Checklist

UPRT enhances pilot resilience and decision-making. Always prioritize early recognition and smooth recovery inputs.

  • ✔ Understand causes of upset
  • ✔ Recognize early warning signs
  • ✔ Apply correct recovery techniques
  • ✔ Practice in simulator scenarios
