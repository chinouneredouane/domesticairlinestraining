Module 7 – Human Factors

⏱ Time: 0m 0s

Human factors play a decisive role in upset recovery. The startle effect, cognitive overload, and delayed reaction times can impair decision-making. Pilots must train to recognize and manage these responses.

Resilience is the ability to recover from unexpected events. It includes:

  • Self-control
  • Adaptability
  • Optimism
  • Self-sufficiency
  • Persistence
